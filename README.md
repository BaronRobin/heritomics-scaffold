# Heritomics scaffold (demo)

A demo of the Creative Science hub, the HS Kaiserslautern (Virtual Design) module within Heritomics. It was built on 22 Oct 2026 to show the team how the hub could work. The final website will live elsewhere, and the look will change.

## What's here

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `demo/` | Clickable demo: the home page and one experience page ("Leben aus Nebel"), filled with research content and photos from Dr. Patrick Jung. Open `demo/index.html` in a browser. |
| `creative-science-starter/` | The starter kit: design guide, design tokens, the original mockup and the scaffold brief. |
| `media/selectedplants/` | Original photos and videos from @selectedplants on Instagram. |

## Fonts

- Poppins and Anton are self-hosted in `demo/fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License. The demo makes no requests to Google Fonts.
- Stratos and Matter, the HS KL brand fonts, are not in this repo. Their web license limits them to HS KL's registered domain and forbids sharing the files. If you have them, unzip `hskl_Fonts.zip` into the repo root (`hskl_Fonts/` is git-ignored) and pick "Marke: Stratos/Matter (nur lokal)" in the demo's font menu.
